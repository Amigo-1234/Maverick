MAVERICK — DESIGN REFERENCE PACK

Contains 9 reference images supplied by the brand owner.

Use these images as visual direction and design exploration, not as artwork to copy.
The references explore:
- Bold, angular, experimental / metal-inspired lettering
- The slogan: “Different By Choice”
- Black and white foundations with blue, yellow and red accent explorations
- Oversized graphic T-shirts and front/back placements
- Sleeve graphics, chest marks and monogram/emblem concepts
- Star and M-based symbol explorations

The goal is to refine these ideas into one coherent, original, wearable and production-ready brand system.
